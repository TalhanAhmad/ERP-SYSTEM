# Investment source rules through F24

Private selected administrators record company holdings and reviewed policy. PENDING explicitly blocks posting; no prices or compliance labels are inferred. Each acquisition creates one immutable original-cost lot. Additional purchases retain separate lots. UNIT uses exact six-decimal quantities; AMOUNT uses carrying amounts or full remaining basis.

Disposal selects explicit lots. Under the holding/configuration lock, the draft captures remaining original cost, carrying value, units and an immutable event-history digest. Partial values round HALF_UP to paisa; final disposal removes the exact remainder. Posting rejects changed lots until the user saves and reviews the draft again. Disposal/value-change dates cannot precede a selected lot's latest movement. This keeps the original reviewed allocation reproducible rather than rewriting later basis.

Gross proceeds less fees is the actual bank debit/credit. Fee expense, removed carrying value and gross realized gain/loss have distinct legs; fees are never subtracted twice. Income receipt recognizes income directly; accrual recognizes a receivable; later settlement clears that receivable with no new income. Negative income-event capacity at any affected date is forbidden.

Impairment and manual valuation affect carrying value, preserving original cost and units. Manual valuation requires a recorded supported policy and explicit approved profit/loss or equity offset. Impairment uses its separate reviewed expense mapping. No automatic prices, rate calculations or trading actions occur.

Corrections post opposite actual journals and dated lot/income events. Consumed acquisitions and corrections that strand value, overrestore original units/cost or produce negative dated balances are rejected. Zero-carrying, zero-cash retirements have immutable source/lot/audit history and no fabricated zero-value journal; their corrections follow the same rule. The shared emitter still verifies fresh access, date and open period for these register-only sources.

Original cost, carrying value, realized disposal result before fees, disposal fees, valuation changes, impairments, earned income and income receivable are displayed separately. All figures are PKR and derived from posted immutable records. Hosted finance remains disabled.
