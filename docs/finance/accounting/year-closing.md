# Fiscal-year close and controlled reversal — F40

## Explicit configuration and review

The Finance Owner configures the actual inclusive fiscal dates, an active PKR posting ordinary equity account (EQUITY/CREDIT, no contra/cash/control mapping), and calendar/mapping evidence. No company fiscal year or retained earnings account is seeded. Configurations cannot overlap another nonvoid year. Dates and mapping remain fixed. An unused mistake can be voided, retained and replaced; a year with closing history cannot be voided. Selected finance administrators can read; other roles cannot access the financial DTOs.

The paged Fiscal years register links to the current preview and immutable annual history. Preview shows the exact proposed income/expense transfer, account codes/names, profit or loss and the final-period checklist. Every configured nonvoid accounting period must cover the fiscal range completely without gaps or straddling boundaries. Earlier periods require genuine F39 reviewed closes; the final period stays OPEN for the annual transfer. Configure the fiscal year before closing its final period. An already closed final period needs a controlled F39 reopen and review before annual close.

Every individual income/expense opening balance before the fiscal start must be zero. Netting a prior revenue credit against an expense debit is insufficient even if total profit is zero. Resolve prior annual closes or an explicitly reviewed cutover first. Earlier configured open fiscal years also block later close. Active posting mappings, past cutoff, 400-line journal limit and all F39 automatic full-company controls apply, including bank/subledger/opening/deferred/register reconciliation, pending posting drafts and unresolved review issues. Both failed-posting and adjustment reviews require explicit owner confirmation and evidence. Automatic historical failed-attempt monitoring remains F42; no previous rolled-back error is fabricated into a review log.

## Atomic close

Fresh provider verification, current owner/grant/session, exact year version, current canonical basis hash and a permanent request key are mandatory. Financial writer/configuration locks serialize posting, configuration and closing. The basis covers reviewed configuration, full statement financial data, controls and all proposed lines. Volatile generation/snapshot metadata and session-dependent creation timestamp formatting are excluded from comparison; retained snapshots preserve their original generation/cutoff evidence.

One transaction creates a numbered annual cycle, transfers each remaining income/expense balance to the reviewed retained-earnings account with sourceType YEAR_CLOSE, captures original annual trial balance/P&L/balance sheet before and after the transfer, and uses F39 to close the final accounting period. Failure rolls back the entire operation and its receipt. The final-period close retains its own statements and export workflow. SQL source/header/line/deferred checks independently bind each annual journal to the exact retained closing lines, actor, dates and cycle. Runtime tables are SELECT-only and unchecked emitters/internal monthly functions have no runtime EXECUTE grant. Ordinary journals cannot directly post to a configured retained-earnings mapping.

Amounts remain exact NUMERIC/decimal strings. Profit credits retained earnings; loss debits it. A year with gross income and expense but zero net profit still clears the individual accounts with a balanced journal. A year with no remaining income/expense balances saves its annual close without manufacturing a zero-value journal. A unique active cycle/source identity and permanent actor/key receipt prevent duplicate posting, including concurrent retries. A changed key body conflicts instead of silently replaying another request.

P&L excludes both YEAR_CLOSE transfers and their opposites. GL and TB include them. The balance sheet combines actual posted equity (including retained earnings) and only the remaining unclosed income/expense result. Closing reduces the latter to zero, so profit is recognized once. Earlier source transactions and all ledger lines remain immutable. No existing journal scalar schema was changed, preserving original typed-source proofs.

## Reopen, correct and reclose

The owner reopens through the annual workflow with the exact year/close versions, fresh verification, permanent key and reason. Reopen later closed periods/years first. The single transaction retains and marks the original annual cycle REVERSED, uses F39 to reopen its final period and supersede affected issued exports/cancel unfinished work, and posts an exact opposite of the original closing journal at the original fiscal end. Empty-year closes have no fake reversal journal. Original source journals, before/after statements, hashes, review evidence and actor/time remain unchanged.

Generic monthly reopening cannot bypass an active annual close. After annual reversal, the final period is open and the original income/expense balances are restored. Reopen earlier affected periods through F39 latest-first when needed, post actual adjustments through their original workflows, then close the earlier periods and run a fresh annual review. Re-closing creates a new numbered cycle and journal; it never overwrites the prior one. Later unclosed books cannot cause duplicate retained profit from an earlier reversed transfer.

Original annual reports remain privately readable with an explicit superseded notice after reversal. Reports and history use 25-row pages; canonical capture includes all rows up to the existing 2,000-row/6MiB report limit and 16MiB per before/after bundle. Oversize capture fails; it never closes on truncated rows. The annual report pages expose saved values; final-period CSV/PDF copies remain available through the linked F39 saved close, subject to current access, expiry and supersession. No annual bearer URL, public file, email or live financial deployment was added.
