# Accounting policies

No company accounting policy has been approved through this implementation. PKR and accrual double entry are build requirements. Fiscal year, cutover, recognition, chart/control mappings, asset threshold/lives, investment measurement, taxes and retention remain [open decisions](../decisions.md).

The supplied examples are isolated acceptance fixtures, never company opening balances. Do not infer payroll from attendance or revenue from task completion. No client email or money transfer is automatically executed.
