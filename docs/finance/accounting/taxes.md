# Tax and withholding source rules (F18)

Tax is optional. No production rates, legal filing rules or compliance labels are seeded. Only the current Finance Owner can create or retire a policy with fresh reauthentication, evidence, a reason and permanent replay receipt. A code has an immutable effective-date version, explicit decimal-string rate (0–1000% supported), inclusive/exclusive mode, HALF_UP or DOWN rounding, and separate input/output controls. Date intervals for a code cannot overlap, even after retirement. Retirement prevents new use while preserving prior snapshots.

Invoice/purchase lines round quantity × rate first, apply the explicit discount, then apply the selected policy. Exclusive tax adds to the discounted net; inclusive tax is extracted from it. PostgreSQL computes authoritative amounts and preserves the code, rate, rounding, dates, evidence and account IDs. Caller-supplied calculated amounts/snapshots are rejected. Source sums, expected GL and mapped accounts are independently checked. Net service amount alone is earned or deferred; sales tax credits its output liability. Purchase tax debits its input control and is separate from expense/prepayment/asset cost. Manual journals cannot use protected tax controls.

Credit requests remain gross and original-line capped. Immutable tax splits allocate each credit between the original net and tax, with cumulative proportional rounding and the final original tax cent retained exactly. The original mapping is used. Deferred credit events contain net only. Taxed backdated credits cannot change already-posted later credit splits: correct those first or choose a later date. Reversals swap the original exact journal and dated events without recalculating the original rate. Nothing rewrites a posted policy snapshot or cash movement.

Receipt gross settlement is cash + explicit fees + evidenced withholding. Supplier payment gross is cash + evidenced withholding (supplier fees remain unsupported/zero). Gross equals dated invoice/bill allocations plus any held advance. Customer withholding debits a recoverable control; supplier withholding credits a payable control. Withholding comes from settlement evidence and company-approved mapping, rather than an automatically inferred statutory calculation. A positive withholding requires a selected applicable code; zero withholding cannot retain a code. A withholding-only settlement has no zero-value cash journal leg. Reversing an original settlement retains its exact withholding snapshot even if the policy is subsequently retired.

All new fields default to no tax/withholding. Optional-null policy and zero-withholding fields are omitted from the canonical request hash, preserving earlier permanent request receipts. An empty line array also has a deterministic non-null digest and is still rejected by the source validator. Live finance remains disabled. Accountant review and real company policy selection are separate release requirements.
