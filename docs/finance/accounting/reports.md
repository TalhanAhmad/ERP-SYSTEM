# Report requirements

Planned GL/trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, direct cash flow, dated AR/AP ageing, project results, funding/asset/investment roll-forwards and budget variance read consistent posted journal/event snapshots. Original entries remain effective before a later reversal; future settlements cannot alter earlier as-of balances. Year-close earnings count once and internal cash transfers are excluded from external cash flow.

No financial report is implemented or approved in F01–F03. Incomplete mappings/reconciliations must block final exports rather than hide differences.
