# F16 — Bills, immediate expenses and cost/project splits

## Goal
Record manual supplier and employee costs once, either on payable terms or paid immediately.

## Task checklist
Separate bill and paid-expense inputs; private supplier/employee parties; cost categories; line project splits; duplicate review; immutable posted source and balanced GL; exact money and current authorization.

## Completed work
Added typed versioned draft/create/edit/void/post commands, searchable register and entry screens. Bills debit expense/prepayment/fixed-asset categories and credit AP, with one dated payable event. Immediate expenses credit the chosen cash master directly, with no AP balance to pay a second time. Supplier references are normalized across both workflows. Reliable references block duplicates; matching supplier/date/amount requires explicit separate-purchase acknowledgement. Original supplier contact snapshot, private audit and permanent replay receipt are atomic. Salary is manually entered using a private employee party; attendance never computes pay.
