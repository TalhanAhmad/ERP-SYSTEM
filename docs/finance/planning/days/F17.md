# F17 — Supplier payments, advances, credits and refunds

## Goal
Settle and correct supplier costs without recognizing the same cost or paying an immediate expense twice.

## Task checklist
Partial/multiple payments; standalone/mixed advances; application; original-cost credits with caps; refund received; dated capacity; concurrency/reversal; current authorization; usable private screens and API contracts.

## Completed work
Added separate supplier document/allocation/balance/credit tables and checked source adapter in the common journal emitter. Payment debits payable/advance asset and credits cash; application clears payable against the original supplier advance without cash; credit reverses the original discounted cost/category, reducing outstanding AP before recording any recoverable supplier balance. Refund received debits cash and clears that asset, with no second expense. Paid-now expenses cannot be paid again. All capacities reserve current and later dated commitments. Source-bound release/reversal journals retain immutable original history, snapshots, audited actions and permanent idempotency receipts. Searchable registers, draft editors, review/post/void/release/reversal screens use private supplier/bill/fund choices. Supplier fees are zero at this stage; no unsupported fee direction is exposed.
